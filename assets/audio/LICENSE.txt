Wave Arena audio asset notice
=============================

The six OGG files in this directory are unmodified selections from:

- Kenney Interface Sounds
  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Kenney Impact Sounds
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Both source packs are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required. Source filenames and local mappings are recorded in /THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md.
